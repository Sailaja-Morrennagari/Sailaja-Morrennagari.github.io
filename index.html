FeaturedAgentic RAG

Local Agentic Copilot

Problem

Cloud copilots send private documents off-device and often answer without showing where facts came from.

Approach

A fully local copilot on Ollama (Qwen3) with Researcher, Coder and Reviewer agents, deterministic guardrails and a bounded self-correction loop.

Evaluation

Reproducible benchmarks compare Top-1 and Top-3 retrieval across semantic, BM25, hybrid and reranked setups, with failure analysis driving each iteration. Final setup: Top-1 45/47 (95.7%) and Hit@3 46/47 (97.9%) on a 47-question evaluation set.

LLMOps Platform

A containerized FastAPI gateway in front of a self-hosted Qwen2.5-3B model, with health and readiness probes and Prometheus metrics for latency, tokens per second and request counts. Benchmarked at p50 2.5 s / p95 6.7 s (20 requests, ~13 tokens/s); output length drives tail latency, and Docker adds no measurable overhead. Next: Helm on Kubernetes, autoscaling, eval-gated CI/CD and Grafana dashboards.
